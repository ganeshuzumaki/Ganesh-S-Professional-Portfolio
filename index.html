02 — Summer Internship Research

Managing the business impact of software change.

A study of the eye-share → SAP Concur ERP transition at GS Shipmanagement (a Genco × Synergy joint venture), carried out from inside the accounts team while the migration was live.

OrganisationGS Shipmanagement · Chennai
PeriodApr – Jun 2026
DesignMixed-method · repeated-measure
ToolsSPSS · Excel · Google Forms

The management dilemma

After go-live, productivity dropped.
Was it a learning curve or a bad fit?

The company moved invoice processing from eye-share, a specialist accounts-payable tool, onto SAP Concur as part of a consolidation onto one SAP platform (Concur + Ariba + FICO). Data migration ran alongside live processing, configuration was incomplete at go-live, and training fell short — creating an invoice backlog and vendor payment delays.

The two diagnoses call for opposite responses: a temporary dip needs patience, while a permanent misfit needs investment. Getting it wrong means either spending money you don't need to, or waiting for a recovery that never comes.

~43vessels in the fleet served by the accounts function
40–60days of vendor payment delay after cutover
23survey responses (≈75% of the team); 18 had used both systems
15 × 4parameters, each rated for eye-share, SAP at cutover, SAP today & importance

My framework

The Three-Force Decomposition Model

"The new system is worse" is really three different complaints. By measuring each parameter at cutover and today, I could tell which force was behind each one.

01

Learning curve

I can't find anything; everything takes longer.
TransientRemedy: time & practice
02

Data-migration defects

TransientRemedy: one-off data clean-up
03

Usability & workflow misfit

It's slower and clumsier than the last one.
PermanentRemedy: reconfiguration

What the data said

Findings

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with effect sizes, importance–performance analysis and Spearman correlations. All scales were 7-point, and four of the five constructs had a Cronbach's α of 0.76–0.96.

So what?

Seven prioritised recommendations

Immediate

  1. Fix system latency — the widest gap between importance and performance; an infrastructure issue, not a training one.
  2. Clean the migrated master data — POs, vendor records and invoice history. Time alone won't fix it.

Medium term

  1. Role-specific training and process guides for each sub-team, so onboarding new joiners stops draining experienced staff.
  2. Retention and reassurance — clear communication before experienced people leave and take their process knowledge with them.

Strategic

  1. SAP SuccessFactors as the HRMS and payroll portal for seafarers.
  2. Extend SAP Ariba to replace the in-house Khaas & Ahoy verification portals.
  3. Close the gap in AI and digital skills so the team can move from transactional work to analytical work.

Verdict

"The destination was correct — it's the transition that needs investment."

Keep the SAP consolidation strategy. Invest in finishing the transition and in the people running it.

Mapping India's EV adoption: a K-Means segmentation of 35 states

VAHAN registration data (MoRTH) · 2021–2025 · Python · scikit-learn

EV adoption in India is very uneven. A single national strategy fits almost no state. We took VAHAN vehicle-registration data for every state and UT and asked a go-to-market question: which states behave alike, and how should each group be targeted?

Using the 2024 EV profile of each state across 9 vehicle categories (from two- and three-wheelers to buses), we standardised the features and ran K-Means++ clustering. The elbow method gave 4 adoption tiers, and we turned each one into a market strategy.

Emerging methods for early detection of forest fires

Python · YOLO real-time object detection · IBM Watson Cloud

Forest fires do their worst damage in the first few minutes, before anyone notices. As part of Nalaiya Thiran, a learning-based industry programme run by the Tamil Nadu government with IBM, we built a system that watches a video feed, detects fire in real time with the YOLO algorithm, and immediately alerts the forest guard on WhatsApp / SMS, so a response can start before the fire spreads.

IBM provided the training dataset and a reference Git repository. We built on them to train, integrate and deploy the detection-to-alert pipeline.

HR Analytics · Machine Learning · Orange Data Mining

Who's about to leave? An attrition prediction model

Logistic Regression · trained on labelled history, scored on current staff

Replacing an employee is expensive, and by the time someone resigns it's too late to act. I built an end-to-end Orange workflow that learns from past employees whose outcome is known (stayed or left), then scores the current, unlabelled workforce so HR can step in early.

Most of the effort went into the data: imputing missing values, removing the noisiest 10% of records with Local Outlier Factor, and one-hot encoding categorical fields such as position, department and recruitment source. Only then did I fit an L2-regularised logistic regression, which keeps the model interpretable, and validate it with stratified k-fold cross-validation and a confusion matrix.

Can India help Nike win back its edge?

Brand, channels and switching among Indian customers

Nike's share of global sports footwear has fallen for three straight years, while Adidas, On and Hoka gained ground.
